ℹ️ How Backbone works

The Big Five

Psychology's best-supported model of personality measures five traits: Openness (curiosity, trying new things), Conscientiousness (follow-through, order, reliability), Extraversion (social energy, initiative), Agreeableness (warmth and cooperation), and Neuroticism (proneness to anxiety and negative emotion). Backbone trains the first four upward and trains the behaviors that pull neuroticism down — shown here as its positive mirror, Emotional Stability.

Traits aren't fixed. Studies on deliberate personality change show that repeatedly acting like the person you want to be — small, concrete behaviors, done often — shifts the traits themselves. That's what the rounds in this game are: reps.

Warmth ≠ doormat

The game deliberately splits healthy agreeableness (warm, fair, cooperative) from doormat behavior (swallowing your needs to avoid conflict). Doormat answers lose points in every culture, and so do aggressive blow-ups. The winning move is always some form of assertiveness: stating what you want, calmly, without apologizing for existing. What changes by culture is the style — blunt-and-brief scores best in direct cultures like Germany or the Netherlands, while firm-but-face-saving scores best in high-context cultures like Japan or Thailand. Same spine, different wrapping.

Anxiety and negative emotion

You can't delete emotions — and you wouldn't want to; anxiety is your alarm system. What you can train is the response: catching distorted thoughts (the CBT skill behind Spot the Distortion), approaching instead of avoiding (the Situations rounds), and downshifting your body (Steady Breath). Do the reps and the alarm fires less often, less loudly, and stops running your life. That's what "low neuroticism" actually looks like from the inside.

Cultures are averages

The country profiles here are broad-brush tendencies drawn from cross-cultural psychology (trait-value surveys, communication-directness research). Real people vary hugely within every culture. Use the profiles as a lens, not a box.

Not therapy

Backbone is a training game, not medical advice. If anxiety or low mood is seriously interfering with your life, a professional (a CBT therapist especially) is the real power-up.
