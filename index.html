attune

a somatic navigation instrument

Your nervous system is always somewhere — wired, foggy, braced, settled. Attune helps you notice where you are, choose where you want to be, and travel the distance in your body, not just your head.

Attune is a practice companion, not medical or psychological care. Everything here is an invitation — nothing is required. If any practice feels like too much, open your eyes, look around the room, and let it go.
