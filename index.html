Stillpoint

Substance over spectacle.

No avatars, no upsells, no pseudoscience. Stillpoint is a small, free toolkit of practices with real evidence behind them — paced breathing, body-scan meditation, and sensory grounding — delivered plainly, tracked privately in your browser.

Paced breathing

Slow, structured breathing engages the parasympathetic nervous system. A 2023 Stanford trial found brief daily "cyclic sighing" (double inhale, long exhale) improved mood and lowered resting respiratory rate more than mindfulness meditation.

Body-scan awareness

Systematically moving attention through the body builds interoception — the ability to notice internal signals early, before they become overwhelm. It's a core component of MBSR, one of the best-studied mindfulness programs.

5–4–3–2–1 grounding

When thoughts spiral, anchoring attention to five senses interrupts the loop. A simple, portable technique widely used in anxiety management and therapy.

Stillpoint is a self-care tool, not medical care. If you're struggling with your mental or physical health, please talk to a qualified professional.

Body scan

A guided pass of attention from feet to crown. Find a comfortable position, seated or lying down. Each region holds for about 30 seconds — roughly six minutes in total. There is nothing to achieve; just notice.

Scan complete

Take one more slow breath and notice how the body feels as a whole.

5–4–3–2–1 grounding

An anchor for racing thoughts. Move through your senses one at a time, naming what you find — out loud or silently. Take it slowly; the naming is the point.

Grounded

You walked your attention through all five senses. Notice whether the noise in your head is a little quieter than when you started.
