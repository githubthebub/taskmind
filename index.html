Full-body states.
No feed required.

Five guided somatic sessions, spoken slowly by a voice that points your attention where it needs to go — breath, weight, warmth, the room. The same mechanisms a good somatic practitioner uses. Each one ends on time and hands you back your evening.

Voices come from your device — nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. Pick the one that sounds most human; on most phones there's at least one good "natural" voice. Headphones help.
Why this app doesn't try to hook you

No account, no streaks, no notifications, no autoplay, no metrics, no infinite anything. Sessions end and say so. The Urge Surf session teaches a skill precisely so that, with practice, you need this app less — that's the point. A tool for your nervous system should behave like a tool: you pick it up, it works, you put it down.

That's the session.

Take a second to notice how your body actually feels — that's data the feed never gives you. If it worked, the best next move is a dark screen.
