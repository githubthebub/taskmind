🪔 About SWARAJ

“Swaraj is when we learn to rule ourselves.” — M.K. Gandhi, Hind Swaraj (1909)

What this is

A life-simulation about the mind India inherited and the one it is building. The colonial hangover is real and measurable — a ~34% wage premium on English, a half-billion-dollar fairness-cream market, a validated psychometric scale for internalized inferiority. But its mirror image — golden-age fantasy and forwarded pseudo-history — is, as Ashis Nandy wrote, still the colonized mind in a different mask. The game's only doctrine is discernment: real pride needs no forgery, and reflexive doubt is not rigor.

The needle

Left is cringe (Fanon's mask, the accent apology). Right is bluster (the WhatsApp aircraft, the purity purge). The centre — swaraj — is not lukewarm; it is self-rule: rooted, evidence-hungry, open to the world without needing its approval.

The receipts are real

Every fact card cites a source: Angus Maddison's GDP tables, Dharampal's British-archive school surveys, the World Bank on remittances, NPCI on UPI, the Lancet on air. Contested numbers ship with their caveats. Full evidence base with citations: docs/RESEARCH.md in this project's repository.

Care

This game contains no communal content and no party politics. Exam-stress storylines are about support, never harm — if you or a friend are struggling, Tele-MANAS 14416 is free, 24×7, in 20 languages.

Privacy

Everything runs on your device. No account, no tracking, no network calls. Your streak and receipts live in your browser only.
