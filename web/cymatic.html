Anchor the ground
110 Hz carrier · 4 Hz binaural

A low tone you feel in the chest, split slightly between the ears. It arrives first and never leaves.

Meaning: you are safe — drop in. Pair it with one long exhale each time you hear it; over many sessions the sound alone brings the settling.

Pīti the rising
inhale-gated · expands in 3D · 440–1760 Hz shimmer

On every in-breath a bright texture blooms wider and higher. The fuller you inhale, the more it opens.

Meaning: gather the energy. It amplifies attention to the tingle of the breath — follow the widening.

Sukha the landing
55 Hz sub-pulse · AUM-like beat · exhale bottom

At the very end of each out-breath, a deep pulse lands and rings out — the floor of the world rising to hold you.

Meaning: rest here. It marks the pause after the exhale, the moment of deepest stillness.

Headphones The Anchor is a true binaural beat — 108 Hz in one ear, 112 Hz in the other. Without stereo separation you'll still hear a pacing tremolo, but the effect is far weaker. Sub-bass also carries better on headphones.
Care Kept deliberately quiet — raise the volume only as far as is comfortable. This is not a medical device and makes no clinical claims. Don't use while driving or operating machinery. If you have epilepsy or are sensitive to rhythmic stimulation, skip it. The session ends in true silence and never loops.
How it works The power is conditioning, not magic: the sounds gain meaning by being paired, session after session, with a genuinely settled breath. Sleep comes from removing arousal — dropping brightness, motion and pacing so your own sleep pressure can take over — not from forcing anything. Full architecture in docs/jhana-sound-architecture.md.
