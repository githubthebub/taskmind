# The Feel-Good Productivity Starter Kit
### by TaskMind

Welcome! This kit contains four tools. Don't try to use all of them at once —
pick the one your quiz result pointed you to, run it for one week, then add the next.

---

## 1. 🗓️ The Weekly Reset (20 minutes, every Sunday)

1. **Empty your head (5 min).** Write down everything on your mind — tasks, worries, ideas. No filtering.
2. **Review last week (5 min).** Ask three questions:
   - What energised me?
   - What drained me?
   - What did I avoid, and why?
3. **Pick 3 weekly priorities (5 min).** Not ten. Three. If everything is a priority, nothing is.
4. **Block them in your calendar (5 min).** A priority without a time slot is a wish.
   Schedule your hardest priority in your highest-energy window.

> Rule of thumb: leave ~20% of your calendar empty. Life will fill it anyway.

---

## 2. 🐸 The Eat-the-Frog Daily Planner

Copy this at the top of each day's notes:

```
FROG (the one task that matters most — do it FIRST):
[ ] ________________________________________

Then, and only then:
[ ] ________________________________________
[ ] ________________________________________

If I finish everything: ______________________
Today's energy level (1–5): ___
```

Rules:
- The frog gets done **before** email, Slack, or meetings whenever possible.
- If a task has lived on your list for 3+ days, it's either not a real task
  (break it down) or not a real priority (delete it).

---

## 3. 🔋 The Energy Audit

For 5 working days, at lunch and at the end of the day, jot down:

| When | What was I doing? | Energy after (+ / 0 / −) |
|------|-------------------|--------------------------|
|      |                   |                          |

After a week, look for patterns:
- **Green tasks (+):** do more of these, and schedule them when you're tired — they recharge you.
- **Red tasks (−):** can you shrink, batch, automate, or delegate them?
  If not, pair them with something pleasant (music, coffee, a walk after).

The goal isn't to eliminate hard work — it's to stop your calendar being
accidentally optimised for exhaustion.

---

## 4. 📵 The Distraction Firewall (one-time setup, ~30 min)

**Phone**
- [ ] Turn off ALL notifications except calls and calendar.
- [ ] Move social/news apps off your home screen (or delete them; browser access adds useful friction).
- [ ] Grayscale mode during work hours.
- [ ] Phone charges outside the bedroom overnight.

**Computer**
- [ ] Close email/chat tabs during focus blocks — open them at set times (e.g. 11:30 and 16:00).
- [ ] Use one browser window per task; bookmark-and-close everything else.
- [ ] Full-screen your work app. Hide the dock/taskbar.

**Environment**
- [ ] Agree "focus hours" with the people around you (headphones = do not disturb).
- [ ] Keep a "distraction pad" next to you: when a random thought hits, write it down and return to work.
- [ ] End each day by setting tomorrow's frog — future-you starts with zero decisions.
