# Chakra Shift

An interactive chakra visualization and attunement game. Open `index.html` in any browser — no build step, no dependencies.

## How it works

1. **Sense** — A meditating body is shown with the seven chakras (Root → Crown). Drag each circle to where you *feel* that energy center sits in your body today, and enlarge or shrink it (size slider, or scroll over a circle) to match how strongly it feels.
2. **Intend** — Say where you *want* each chakra to be. Drag the dashed target rings into place and size them, or snap all targets back to the classic spinal alignment with one click.
3. **Shift** — A breath-guided game moves each chakra from where you feel it toward where you intend it. Press and hold during **inhale**, release for **exhale**; better breath timing produces a stronger shift each cycle. Reach 100% harmony to complete the attunement.

Per-chakra alignment bars and an overall harmony score track progress. Your layout is saved locally in the browser, and touch input is supported.

*A reflective visualization for intention-setting — not medical or therapeutic advice.*
