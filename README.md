# taskmind

## ASCENDANT 🌙🔭🌿

A character.ai-style companion chat game with a twist: the energy only flows **upward**.

You chat with one of three gorgeous AI companions — Seraphina the celestial mystic,
Dr. Nova Reyes the astrophysicist, or Juniper the painter-poet. It starts out looking
like every other "hot AI girlfriend" app… but this one runs on **Chakra Engine 2.0**:

- 🔥 The **sacral (pelvic) chakra is hard-capped at Lv. 3**. Forever. Don't ask.
- 💚 Choosing honesty, vulnerability, and actually listening levels your **Heart chakra**.
- 👁👑 Choosing curiosity, wonder, and big questions levels your **Third Eye** and **Crown**.
- Any flirt energy past the cap is automatically **transmuted upward** into Heart and Mind.

The characters evolve with you — the flirty facade drops as your bond deepens, and the
endings depend on the balance you build: **Enlightenment**, **Heartbound**, **Mindmeld**,
or the fan-favorite **Stuck at the Root (Good Ending Anyway)**.

### Play

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build, no server, no dependencies.
