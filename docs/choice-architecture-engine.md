# The Choice Architecture Engine (CAE)

**A self-directed mental interface for redirecting high-arousal cognitive load into attentional absorption or sleep.**

Version 1.0 — Interface copy, instructional dialogue, and protocol specification.

---

## Design Notes (read once, then ignore)

- **What this is:** a decision-support and attention-training script the practitioner runs *in their own mind*, optionally supported by an audio track (Module 3). The "engine" is the practitioner's prefrontal cortex; the "interface" is rehearsed language and imagery.
- **On the numbers:** the Outcome Forecasts use quantified language ("baseline −40%", "accessibility 0%") because concrete numbers recruit the brain's loss-aversion machinery far better than vague warnings. They are *decision-weighting heuristics*, directionally grounded in the relevant literature (post-orgasmic prolactin release, refractory dopamine suppression, DMN activity during craving vs. focused attention) — not clinical measurements of this practitioner. Treat them as the engine's internal currency, not lab values.
- **Core mechanism:** urges are not suppressed. They are *outbid*. A high-arousal visual fantasy wins attention because it is the highest-stimulation content currently on offer. The engine's job is to put a competing task on the table that is (a) visually and computationally expensive enough to occupy the same cortical real estate, and (b) free of the neurochemical debt. This is stimulus substitution, not willpower.
- **Safety rails:** nothing here overrides medical care. Breath counts are gentle (4-in / 6-out); anyone with cardiovascular or respiratory conditions should keep breathing comfortable rather than exact. If intrusive sexual thoughts are compulsive or distressing at a clinical level, this tool is an adjunct, not a substitute for a clinician.

# MODULE 1 — The Dynamic Dashboard & Outcome Forecast

**When to run:** the instant a high-arousal visual trigger or fantasy loop is detected forming. Latency matters; the dashboard must be invoked inside the first 5–10 seconds, before the imagery acquires narrative momentum.

**Invocation cue (memorized, said internally, flat tone):**

> **"Dashboard up. Three paths. Forecast running."**

This sentence is the interrupt. It does not fight the fantasy; it simply opens a second window on top of it. The practitioner then reads (from memory) the following interface:

---

## ┌─ CHOICE ARCHITECTURE ENGINE — SESSION START ─┐

**System status detected:**
- Baseline charge: **HIGH** (≈90 days retained energetic surplus)
- Novelty-seeking drive: **ACTIVE** — currently proposing high-arousal visual content
- Time of day: [evening/night assumed] — sleep window opens in < 90 min

**The drive is not the enemy. It is unallocated processing power requesting a task.
Three tasks are available. Review the forecasts. Allocate deliberately.**

---

### ▸ PATH A — The High-Arousal Fantasy Loop
*"Run the vivid imagery to completion."*

**OUTCOME FORECAST — PATH A**

| System | T+0 to T+15 min | T+15 min to T+8 hrs |
|---|---|---|
| **Dopamine baseline** | Spike well above baseline during the loop | Crash **below** starting baseline; receptor downregulation; tomorrow's ordinary tasks rendered flat and unrewarding |
| **Prolactin / opioid cascade** | — | Post-climax prolactin surge: lethargy, mental fog, motivational shutdown of exactly the drive you've spent 3 months consolidating |
| **Default Mode Network** | DMN fully captured by self-referential narrative; loop strengthens the trigger→fantasy pathway (each rep lowers next activation threshold) | DMN remains sticky and ruminative; the same imagery returns *stronger* tomorrow |
| **Jhana / absorption access** | — | **0%.** Absorption requires a stable, energized attentional system. The prolactin–dopamine trough removes both the stability and the energy. Access window closed for ~24–48 hrs |
| **Sleep efficiency** | Possible fast sleep onset from the crash | Fragmented architecture; reduced slow-wave proportion; wake-up with the surplus *gone* but nothing purchased with it |

**Net position:** you spend 90 days of accumulated charge on 15 minutes of simulation and buy a 24–48 hr lockout of your primary training objective. **The engine does not forbid this path. It prices it.**

---

### ▸ PATH B — The Attentional Anchor Matrix (AAM)
*"Spend the charge on a high-load geometric simulation."*

**OUTCOME FORECAST — PATH B**

| System | T+0 to T+20 min | After |
|---|---|---|
| **Dopamine baseline** | Moderate, sustained engagement reward (novelty + task difficulty) with **no spike-crash geometry** | Baseline preserved or slightly raised; interest in tomorrow's work intact |
| **Default Mode Network** | Progressively **suppressed** — the rendering task is externally-structured and attention-demanding; DMN and task-positive network are mutually inhibitory. The fantasy loses its host process | DMN quieted; trigger→fantasy pathway receives a *non-reinforcement* rep, weakening it |
| **Jhana / absorption access** | **Rising.** The tesseract is a kasina-class visual anchor; sustained single-pointed rendering is the on-ramp to access concentration | If absorption arises, ride it. If not, hand off to Module 4 for sleep |
| **Sleep efficiency** | — | High. Charge discharged as somatic heat and muscular release, not neurochemical debt |

**Cost:** ~15–20 minutes of genuinely hard attentional work. This path is expensive up front and cheap forever after.

---

### ▸ PATH C — The Pavlovian Sound-Somatic Matrix (PSSM)
*"Let conditioned audio drive the body directly. No visualization required."*

**OUTCOME FORECAST — PATH C**

| System | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Dopamine baseline** | Neutral-to-positive; auditory novelty satisfies the seeking drive at low cost |
| **Autonomic state** | Direct vagal activation via conditioned sigh-response; heart-rate variability up, muscle tone down |
| **Default Mode Network** | Bypassed rather than suppressed — non-verbal input gives the narrative engine *nothing to narrate* |
| **Jhana / absorption access** | Moderate; PSSM is optimized for downshift, not peak absorption |
| **Sleep efficiency** | **Highest of the three paths.** Choose C when cognitive fuel is too low to render Path B's geometry |

**Cost:** requires the audio track and prior conditioning reps (see Module 3). Near-zero effort once trained.

---

## ┌─ SELECTION LOGIC ─┐

> **IF** mind is electric, fantasy is vivid, and you have ≥ 15 min → **PATH B.**
> **IF** body is tired but mind won't stop narrating → **PATH C.**
> **IF** you are choosing Path A → re-read its forecast **one time, slowly**, then choose freely. A choice made with the invoice visible is architecture. A choice made without it is a reflex.

**Confirm selection by saying it internally:** *"Allocating to B."* / *"Allocating to C."*
The act of verbal allocation transfers control from limbic proposal to prefrontal execution.

# MODULE 2 — The Attentional Anchor Matrix (AAM)

**Word-for-word instructional script. Read aloud into a recording, or memorize the section headers and self-instruct. Delivery: measured, technical, unhurried — a flight controller, not a spa.**

---

### Phase 0 — Position & Boot Sequence (60 seconds)

> "Lie on your back or sit upright. Hands open, palms down on thighs or bed. Eyes closed.
>
> Do not try to relax. Relaxation is a downstream output; we are upstream. Your only job for the next fifteen minutes is *rendering*.
>
> Notice the charge in your body — the heat, the restlessness, the pressure. Do not argue with it. That is your fuel gauge reading FULL. We are about to route that fuel to the visual cortex.
>
> Behind your closed eyes there is a dark field. Grainy, shifting, imperfect. That is your canvas. Accept the graininess; you are not aiming for photorealism, you are aiming for *engagement of the machinery*."

### Phase 1 — Structure Instantiation (2 minutes)

> "In the center of the dark void, place a single point of pale blue-white light.
>
> From that point, extrude a cube. Thin, luminous edges — like fiber-optic filament. Twelve edges, eight vertices. Hold it. If it wobbles or dissolves, rebuild it without comment. Every rebuild is a repetition, not a failure.
>
> Now the expensive step. Around your cube, project a second, larger cube — and connect every vertex of the inner cube to its counterpart on the outer cube. Eight connecting struts. You are now holding a tesseract projection: a four-dimensional lattice compressed into your three-dimensional workspace. It should feel *slightly too hard to hold*. That is correct. That difficulty is the entire mechanism — a structure complex enough that rendering it leaves no spare cycles for any other imagery.
>
> Assign it a material: crystalline. Edges refract faintly, like quartz under moonlight. Interior: empty, black, cold.
>
> Set it rotating. Slowly. The inner cube rotates one way; the outer shell counter-rotates. If your mind can hold only one rotation, hold one. Load should sit at the edge of your capacity — never comfortably inside it."

### Phase 2 — Coupling the Breath Engine (core loop, ~8–10 minutes)

> "The structure now gets connected to your breath. Three linked commands per cycle. Learn them in slow motion first; they will fuse within four or five cycles.
>
> **COMMAND ONE — THE HIGH-RESOLUTION INHALE. Four seconds.**
> As you draw the breath in through the nose — deep, into the lower ribs — the tesseract responds to the incoming energy:
> — Its edges **brighten**, blue-white flaring toward white, as if your inhale is current flowing into the lattice.
> — Its rotation **accelerates**, smoothly, like a flywheel receiving torque.
> — Its resolution **sharpens** — you can suddenly see the fine refraction along each crystalline edge.
> The inhale is not near the structure. The inhale **powers** the structure. Four seconds of charging.
>
> **COMMAND TWO — THE SOMATOSENSORY SHIFT. At the top of the inhale, hold for two seconds and squeeze.**
> Contract the large muscle groups — **quadriceps, glutes, core** — at roughly sixty percent of maximum. Not a strain; a firm, deliberate clench.
> Feel what this does mechanically: the big muscles are now the highest-demand tissue in the body. Blood flow and raw heat are being *pulled out of the pelvic floor* and re-tasked into the thighs, the hips, the abdominal wall. The arousal charge is not being fought. It is being **outcompeted for circulation**. Two seconds. The tesseract holds at maximum brightness and speed, locked, blazing.
>
> **COMMAND THREE — THE RADIANT EXHALE. Six seconds.**
> Release the squeeze and exhale slowly through the nose or pursed lips.
> As the muscles let go, track the heat they generated: it does not sink back down. It **radiates outward** — down the thighs, past the knees, out through the **soles of the feet**; along the arms, out through the **palms of the hands**. Map it precisely: warmth flowing to the four exits and leaving as it goes, so that what remains behind it is **heavy, cool, and tranquil**. Limbs gaining weight with every centimeter the heat travels outward.
> Simultaneously the tesseract **dims and decelerates** — edges settling from white back to deep blue, rotation easing to a slow drift. The structure is exhaling with you.
> This spreading, heavy, pleasant coolness has a name in the absorption literature: **sukha**. You are not imagining it into existence; you are noticing what a discharging nervous system actually feels like, and giving attention a place to rest on it.
>
> **The full cycle: four seconds charging the lattice — two seconds squeeze-and-lock — six seconds radiant release. Twelve seconds. Run it again. And again. Count nothing. The structure is your counter.**"

### Phase 3 — Absorption Gate (open-ended)

> "Somewhere between cycle ten and cycle thirty, one of two things happens.
>
> **Either** the tesseract begins to hold itself — rendering cost drops, brightness stabilizes, the breath couples automatically, and a quiet, unified pleasantness spreads through the body. If so: stop operating the controls. Let the structure slow to a near-stop, keep attention resting on it lightly, and allow the absorption to deepen on its own. You are at the access gate. Do not grab at it; grabbing is the only way to lose it.
>
> **Or** fatigue arrives — the structure gets heavy to render and the eyes-behind-the-eyes feel thick. That is not failure; that is the mission's second success condition. Proceed directly to the Sleep Downshift, Module 4."

# MODULE 3 — The Pavlovian Sound-Somatic Matrix (PSSM)

**A non-verbal conditioning interface. Language is deliberately excluded: words feed the narrative engine (DMN); pure acoustics feed the brainstem and body. The track speaks in a three-word sound vocabulary, and each "word" is trained to trigger a physical response automatically.**

## 3.1 The Audio Specification (build or commission this track)

| Layer | Specification | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **L1 — Sub-bass drone** | **110 Hz** sine/soft-saw drone, entering as a **sudden drop** from silence or from a higher pad; felt in the chest as much as heard. Sustained 45–90 s per occurrence | The **command word for the body**: vagal trigger + immobility lock (see 3.2) |
| **L2 — Spatialized chimes** | Sparse crystalline chimes (2–4 kHz partials), binaurally **spatialized in 3D** — one far left-high, next right-low, next behind the head; irregular intervals of 8–20 s | Feeds the novelty/seeking system a clean stimulus: the brain's *where-is-it* orienting reflex gets satisfied without any narrative or imagery. This is the dopamine proxy layer |
| **L3 — Delta binaural bed** | Carrier ~100 Hz left / **104 Hz** right → **4 Hz** binaural beat (delta band), mixed low beneath L1/L2, **slowly fading up over 20 min as L2 chimes fade out and spread apart** | Entrainment scaffold and session clock: as chime density falls and the delta bed rises, stimulation tapers toward sleep |

**Track arc (20 min):** 0–2 min ambient pad → **first 110 Hz drop** → chime field active, drone recurring every ~2 min → from min 12, chimes thin out, drone intervals lengthen, delta bed rises → final 3 min: drone and delta only → silence.

## 3.2 The Conditioning Protocol — training the sound language

A Pavlovian link is built by *pairing*, not by understanding. Run this training nightly for 7–10 sessions; after that the responses fire on their own.

> **THE DRONE = "BODY: DOWN."**
> Every single time the 110 Hz drop occurs — no exceptions, this consistency *is* the training — execute two actions immediately:
>
> 1. **The physiological sigh:** a double inhale through the nose — one full inhale, then a short second sip of air stacked on top — followed by a long, passive exhale through the mouth. This maneuver maximally reinflates collapsed alveoli and produces the fastest known voluntary activation of the vagal brake: heart rate drops within seconds. One sigh per drone onset. Two if the charge is high.
> 2. **The immobility lock:** on the exhale, release *all* voluntary movement. No swallowing adjustments, no finger drift, no postural correction — the body becomes an object lying in a field of sound. Total stillness is itself a powerful sleep signal: motionlessness is the body's evidence that the environment is safe and the muscles are off-shift.
>
> **THE CHIMES = "ATTENTION: FETCH."**
> When a chime sounds, let attention flick to its location in 3D space — left-high, right-low, behind — and simply *find* it. Do not name it, rate it, or wait for the next one. Locate, release. Each fetch is a micro-dose of the same orienting satisfaction the fantasy imagery was offering, at one-thousandth the cost. The body does **not** move during fetches; the immobility lock stays engaged. Only attention travels.
>
> **THE DELTA BED = "SYSTEM: DESCEND."**
> The 4 Hz beat is never attended to directly. It is the floor. As the chimes grow sparse you will notice the floor rising; when you can no longer be bothered to fetch a chime, the interface has completed its handover and you let go entirely.

**Why this works when words don't:** by night ten, the 110 Hz drop produces the heart-rate dip *before* you consciously execute the sigh — the reflex has moved below language. You now possess a remote control for your own vagus nerve, and it works precisely on the nights when you are too depleted to visualize anything at all.

# MODULE 4 — The Parasympathetic Sleep Downshift

**Entry conditions:** from AAM Phase 3 fatigue branch, from the end of the PSSM track, or standalone in bed. The principle: attention was captured with high complexity; it is now walked down a *complexity staircase* so that when the structure finally vanishes, there is nothing left for the mind to hold onto except breath and descent.**

### Step 1 — Dim the Lines (≈2 minutes)

> "The tesseract — or, if arriving from Path C, a single softly-lit cube — hangs in the void, drifting slowly. Reduce edge luminance by half. The blazing filament becomes an ember-line, deep blue, barely distinct from the dark. Rendering cost falls with the brightness. Let it."

### Step 2 — Lock the Axes (≈2 minutes)

> "Halt the rotation. One axis at a time: the counter-rotating shell first, then the inner cube. The structure comes to rest, perfectly still in the void — and notice that as it stops moving, so do you. The immobility lock from the sound training engages here whether or not audio is playing: total voluntary stillness. A stationary object requires almost nothing to render. You are now holding a dim, frozen lattice with a nearly idle visual cortex."

### Step 3 — Dissolve to Mist (≈2 minutes)

> "Release the geometry. Vertices soften. Edges lose their lines. The lattice de-resolves into a **formless, faintly luminous mist** — no shape, no boundary, no features to track. Give the mist exactly one property: it **pulses**. A slow brightening on every inhale, a slow dimming on every exhale — nothing more. Attention now has one bit of information left to process. This is the last handhold above sleep."

### Step 4 — The Adenosine Countdown (until sleep)

> "Now the final protocol. You have been running expensive cognitive machinery all day, and for the last twenty minutes you deliberately spent down what remained. Sleep pressure — the adenosine load your neurons have been accruing every waking hour — is fully banked and pressing. The countdown does not create sleep; it removes the last process standing between you and pressure that is already there.
>
> Breathe naturally. No counts, no ratios — the 4/6 engine is off. Just the breath the body chooses.
>
> At the **bottom of each exhale** — in that slack, silent pause before the next inhale begins — place one number into the mist. Start at **twenty**.
>
> Exhale... bottom... *twenty*. The mist dims a step.
> Exhale... bottom... *nineteen*. Dimmer.
>
> The numbers are not counted *toward* anything. Each one is an unhooking — one more notch of muscle tone released, one more degree of dimness, one step further into the descent. The pause at the bottom of the exhale is where the parasympathetic system holds the controls; you are stamping each release at the exact moment of maximum vagal tone.
>
> If you lose count, you are succeeding. Take whatever number floats up — or start at twenty again. The rules stopped mattering several numbers ago.
>
> By the low teens the numbers arrive slower, from further away.
> Somewhere below ten, the mist and the counter and the body are handed over entirely to the pressure that has been waiting all day —
> and the system completes its shutdown without you."
