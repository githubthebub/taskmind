## 1. What kind of mentality do Indians have right now?

### 1.1 World-leading optimism — but future-loaded and unevenly distributed

- **66–73% of Indians say the country is on the right track** (Ipsos *What Worries the World*, 2025–26 waves) — consistently top-4 globally, vs. single digits in parts of Europe.
- **India ranked #1 in the Ipsos Global Happiness Index 2025** (88% say they're happy; top drivers: family and "feeling loved") — yet ranked **118th of 147 in the World Happiness Report 2025**, below Pakistan and Nepal. The gap is *who gets asked* (urban online panels vs. national probability samples). "The Indian mindset" is really several mindsets.
- WHR's lowest Indian sub-score: **perceived freedom to make life choices** — despite top-tier family support. A society rich in belonging, poor in felt agency.
- **RBI Consumer Confidence Survey**: Current Situation Index ~96–98 (below the neutral 100) while Future Expectations sits at ~125. **Present pain, future faith.**
- Trust in institutions is very high (Edelman 2025: India trust index 75, 79% trust government), but Edelman 2026 recorded a **13-point fall in "next generation will be better off."**

### 1.2 Rising-nation conviction

- **68% of Indians say India's influence in the world is getting stronger** — vs. a 28% median across 19 other surveyed countries (Pew, 2023). Rural residents are *more* bullish (72%) than urban (62%).
- **72% completely agree "Indian culture is superior to others"** (Pew *Religion in India*, 2019–20). **95% of Indian Muslims say they are very proud to be Indian** — national pride is cross-communal.
- Pride events function as shared morale infrastructure: Chandrayaan-3's south-pole landing (2023), UPI's global spread, the G20 presidency.

### 1.3 The jobs anxiety and the sarkari naukri paradox

- Youth are **~83% of India's unemployed**; the educated share of the unemployed doubled from 35% (2000) to **66% (2022)** (ILO/IHD India Employment Report 2024). Education raises expectations faster than opportunities.
- **~3 in 5 young Indians prefer a government job** (CSDS-Lokniti Youth Study 2023); 80% cite security, 86% pensions (Genius Consultants 2025). The odds are brutal and known: **3.59 crore candidates for 92,116 railway vacancies** (RRB 2024); UP Police 2024 ran ~80:1.
- Yet preference for running one's own business grew **16% (2007) → 27% (2023)** (CSDS). The mindset isn't shifting from security to ambition — **it's layering both**.
- Exam pressure is lethal: **student suicides rose from 7,696 (2011) to 14,488 (2024)** (NCRB); Kota recorded 32 in 2023. Any game touching this must do so with care, never depiction.
- Rural youth mostly do **not** dream of the metro: **60–70% would take a 20–30% pay cut to stay near their village** (DIU/Transform Rural India 2024). Aspiration is for dignity near home.

### 1.4 The three Indias

Blume Ventures' *Indus Valley Report 2025*: **"India1"** (~140M people, ~$15k per-capita) does ~66% of discretionary spending; **"India2"** (~300M, ~$3k) are "heavy consumers, reluctant payers"; **"India3"** (~1B) lives at Sub-Saharan-Africa income levels. Different psychological economies in one country.

### 1.5 Emigration is class-forked and cooling

- Willingness to work abroad **fell from 78% (2020) to 54% (2023)** (BCG). Student outflows fell 15% in 2024 (MEA).
- But **1 in 5 ultra-wealthy Indians is migrating or planning to** (Kotak *Top of the Pyramid* 2024); ~4,300 millionaires left in 2024 (Henley).
- Students going abroad now come 57% from Tier-2/3 cities (UpGrad 2024–25) — the aspiration spreads down the ladder as the elite tire of it.

## 2. The colonial hangover — documented, not imagined

### 2.1 Language as a caste of its own

- English fluency carries a **~34% hourly wage premium** — equal to the return on completing secondary school (Azam, Chin & Prakash, IZA/World Bank). Premiums reach **149–155%** for older professionals.
- Only **~10.6% of Indians** reported speaking English (Census 2011), yet English-medium enrollment nearly doubled in five years (15M → 29M students, 2008–2014, British Council). English is a scarce, high-return, socially coded gatekeeper.
- Macaulay's *Minute on Indian Education* (2 Feb 1835) is the canonical origin document: education funding redirected to produce "a class of persons, Indian in blood and colour, but English in taste, in opinions, in morals, and in intellect."

### 2.2 Colorism, monetized

- India is the world's largest skin-lightening market (**~$450–535M around 2020**); Fair & Lovely alone earned **>$500M/year** before its 2020 rename to "Glow & Lovely" (Fortune; NBC).
- Peer-reviewed studies of matrimonial ads (SAGE Open 2018; Duke; a 2010–2024 computational study of 30 newspapers) consistently find fairness demanded far more of brides than grooms — colorism is gendered and priced.

### 2.3 "Foreign = better" — real, and reversing

- 1.34M Indian students abroad (2024, NITI Aayog), spending ~₹2.9 trillion (~$35B) a year — projected toward $70B (RedSeer/ICEF) — an education *import bill* already rivaling India's entire inbound-tourism earnings (~$35B, Ministry of Tourism 2024).
- But the premium is eroding: ~1/3 of surveyed employers won't hire fresh foreign-degree graduates; **~45% of returnees say the degree didn't help** land their first job (PIE News; Business Standard).
- Mintel: **Indian brands now outpoll imported ones 2:1+** across clothing, personal care, packaged food. The "imported = superior" reflex is documented as *weakening* — the game rides a real trend, it doesn't invent one.

### 2.4 Institutional relics

- The **1860 Indian Penal Code governed India until 1 July 2024** (replaced by the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita); the **Police Act of 1861** still structures policing; courts retain "My Lord" (a 2006 Bar Council resolution called it "a relic of the colonial past" — ~70% of surveyed lawyers still use it).
- Symbolic decolonization is ongoing and cross-cutting: Rajpath → Kartavya Path, the Navy ensign shedding the St George's Cross (2022), 1,500+ obsolete colonial laws repealed. Legal scholars note the twist: replacements sometimes preserve the old logic (NLSIU on BNS §152 reproducing sedition in broader form). **Renaming is not always rewiring** — a nuance the game encodes.

### 2.5 It's measurable psychology, not just discourse

- The **Colonial Mentality Scale** (David & Okazaki 2006), adapted for Asian Indians (CMS-AI), measures five subscales: *cultural shame, internalized inferiority, within-group discrimination, physical-characteristics preference (skin tone), colonial debt*. A 2021 study (n=198) found **32% endorsement** and correlation with depressive symptoms.
- Ashis Nandy, *The Intimate Enemy* (1983): colonialism's "second colonization" — of minds — "survived the end of empires." His trap: the militant who fights the colonizer by mirroring him **is still colonized**.

## 3. The decolonial canon — and its guardrails

### 3.1 Two rails

**Economic rail (the receipts):**
- **Dadabhai Naoroji** quantified the drain (~£30–40M/yr by 1901) and made arguably the world's first poverty-line calculation: per-capita income Rs. 20 vs. Rs. 34 subsistence (1867–68).
- **Angus Maddison** (OECD 2001): India's share of world GDP **~24.4% in 1700 → 4.2% in 1950**.
- **Utsa Patnaik** (2017): $44.6T drain 1765–1938 — *headline is mostly compound interest at 5%; methodologically contested* (see §3.3).
- **Dharampal, *The Beautiful Tree*** (1983), from British survey records: **11,575 schools and 1,094 colleges in Madras Presidency (1822–25)**; in Tamil districts, Shudras and below formed **70–80% of pupils** — colonial archives themselves contradict the "only Brahmins were educated" story. (Girls, however, were largely absent — Dharampal conceded this.)

**Psychological-cultural rail (the mirror):**
- **Fanon** (*Black Skin, White Masks*, 1952): internalized inferiority; the mask worn for the colonizer's approval estranges the self.
- **Ngũgĩ wa Thiong'o** (*Decolonising the Mind*, 1986): the "cultural bomb" — annihilating a people's belief in their names, languages, and ultimately themselves. Language carries culture.
- **Coomaraswamy** (1918): "A single generation of English education suffices to break the threads of tradition... a sort of intellectual pariah who does not belong to the East or the West."
- **Gandhi** (*Hind Swaraj*, 1909): swaraj (self-rule) begins in the mind; swadeshi as self-reliance, not mere boycott.
- **Tagore** (*Nationalism*, 1917): the indispensable counterweight — rejects both servility ("not tutelage under European schoolmasters") *and* the Nation as machine. "True modernism is freedom of mind, not slavery of taste."

### 3.2 The 2015 viral moment

Shashi Tharoor's Oxford Union reparations speech (May 2015) went viral in India months later and was praised across party lines — evidence that decolonial framing has mass-culture traction. Core stat: India's world-economy share ~23% → ~4% under British rule.

### 3.3 Where decolonial tips into chauvinism — the game's guardrails

- **Tirthankar Roy's caution**: a falling *share* of world GDP reflects the West's industrial explosion, not proof India was "rich" then impoverished in absolute terms. The game quotes Maddison with this nuance.
- **The $45T figure is mostly compound interest** — rhetorically potent, methodologically fragile. Quote with caveat or prefer Naoroji's contemporaneous arithmetic.
- **Gandhi lost the receipts battle but won the metaphor war**: he conceded to Sir Philip Hartog (1931–39 exchange) that he couldn't substantiate "India is more illiterate than 50 years ago." Enrollment ≠ literacy; Adam's "100,000 schools of Bengal" is definitional, not settled.
- **The 2015 Indian Science Congress "Vedic interplanetary aircraft" fiasco**: the *Vaimanika Shastra* was debunked by IISc aeronautical engineers **in 1974** (composed 1900–1922, not ancient). The canonical cautionary tale of pseudo-history wearing decolonial clothes.
- **Other documented fabrications in wide circulation** (both used as counterfeit "Forwards" in the game): the viral *"Macaulay 1835: I have not seen one beggar in India"* quote — a fabrication that contradicts his real Minute — and the *"Agastya Samhita battery/electroplating"* claim, whose text dating is contested and late, with no archaeological support.
- **Meera Nanda's warning**: treating scientific rationality itself as "colonial" hands the vocabulary to pseudoscience.
- **Olúfẹ́mi Táíwò** (*Against Decolonisation*, 2022): rejecting ideas merely for colonial provenance denies the colonized their **agency to adopt, adapt, or reject**. Decolonization-1 (self-rule: good) ≠ decolonization-2 (provenance-purging: self-harm).
- **Nandy's mirror-trap** (1983): hyper-masculine counter-nationalism that mirrors the colonizer is still colonization. The canon contains its own antidote.

**Design consequence: the game's virtue is *discernment*, not provenance.** India's real receipts (zero, wootz steel, Madhava's series, Sushruta's rhinoplasty, Maddison's tables, UPI) are impressive enough that forged ones (Vedic aircraft) are an insult to them. *Real pride needs no forgery.*

## 4. The levers — how individual mindset compounds into national power

### 4.1 Soft power (huge, under-captured)

| Lever | Numbers | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Yoga/wellness | Global market **~$125.8B (2025)**; India's domestic share **~$5B (~4%)**. 260M participants in Yoga Day 2025 | Expert Market Research; Grand View |
| Cinema | "Naatu Naatu" — first Oscar for a fully Indian production (2023); RRR ~$155M worldwide; **25% of Prime Video's Indian-content viewing is now international** | CNN; Media Play News |
| Cuisine | Global Indian-restaurant market **~$78.4B (2024)** | Growth Market Reports |
| Diaspora | **$129.1B remittances in 2024** — highest share (14.3%) any country has held since 2000; 11 Fortune-500 CEOs of Indian heritage steering **$6.5T combined market cap** | World Bank; Newsweek |
| UPI / India Stack | **20B+ transactions/month (2025)**; ~**50% of the world's real-time digital payments**; live in 7–8 countries; MOSIP (open-source ID) powering ~90M IDs across 11 countries | NPCI; IMF; Carnegie |
| ISRO | 397 foreign satellites launched in a decade; Chandrayaan-3 cost **~$75M — less than many Hollywood films**; 400+ space startups | WION; WEF |

**The recurring pattern — and the game's core tension: India generates enormous cultural value but must fight to *capture* it** (a $125B yoga economy of which India keeps 4%).

### 4.2 Hard power (indigenizing fast)

- Defense exports up **~34x in 11 years** to ₹23,622 crore (FY2024-25), reaching 100+ countries; BrahMos to the Philippines ($375M, first export).
- **~25% of the world's iPhones assembled in India (2025)**; the iPhone became **India's single largest export item (~$23B)** — smartphones displaced petroleum as the top export category.
- First Micron chips from Sanand (2025); defense indigenization at ~65%; renewables 3rd globally (250+ GW), world's #2 solar growth market.
- GDP: **4th-largest economy in 2025** (passed Japan, IMF), 3rd projected ~2028–2030.

### 4.3 Quality of life (the unglamorous plumbing)

- Swachh Bharat sanitation: **300,000+ deaths averted** by 2019 (WHO estimate) — the purest individual-behavior → national-outcome lever in the dataset.
- Jan Dhan: **550M accounts (56% women-held)**; DBT plugged **₹3.48 lakh crore of leakage** (2009–2024).
- Jal Jeevan: rural tap-water coverage **17% (2019) → ~82% (2026)** — freeing hours of (mostly women's) daily fetching time.
- Female labor-force participation: **23.3% (2017-18) → 41.7% (2023-24)** (PLFS) — with the caveat that much is unpaid/self-employment. Closing the gender work gap is India's single largest untapped growth lever.
- **The anti-lever: air.** PM2.5 kills ~1.72M Indians/year and costs **$339B ≈ 9.5% of GDP** (Lancet Countdown 2025); average life expectancy cut by **3.5 years** (AQLI). India loses more than a year's growth to bad air, every year — and it is substantially an aggregation of individual choices (stubble, crackers, fuel, dust).

## 5. What works in games that change minds

- **Effects are real but modest**: implicit attitudes g≈0.36, explicit g≈0.24 (Kolek et al. 2023); behavior g≈0.26 (DeSmet et al. 2014). **Multi-session exposure is the strongest moderator** → build a daily ritual, not a one-shot message game.
- **Procedural rhetoric** (Bogost 2007): the argument must live in the rules. *September 12th* proved a feedback loop can make players discover a thesis. *Papers, Please* made empathy via **complicity** — mercy has a mechanical price.
- **Anti-preachiness is quantified**: Kaufman & Flanagan's Embedded Design — a bias game with **~45% on-message content beat a 75% version**. Intermix, obfuscate, distance. "Chocolate-covered broccoli" (Laurel) and psychological reactance are the documented killers.
- **The agency trap** (Roussos & Dovidio 2016, *Spent*): giving players full control over structurally-determined outcomes teaches "it's their own fault." Calibrate agency; let constraint be felt.
- **Transparent causality** (*Democracy 3*'s focus groups): showing *why* an outcome happened converts play into systems thinking.
- **Micro-copy is a mechanic**: renaming "Inventory" → "Our Things" measurably changed player behavior (*This War of Mine*).
- **Streaks build identity, not just retention** (Duolingo: D1 retention 12%→55% over years of iteration): "I'm a person who keeps streaks" → "I'm a person who thinks like a builder."
- **Wordle's loop**: one shared daily puzzle + a spoiler-free emoji share artifact = appointment behavior + status ritual.
- **India-specific**: 2–5 minute one-thumb sessions; <20–30MB installs; offline-first; low-RAM; icon-led UI. Data is cheap (~₹9/GB); devices and typing Indic scripts are the real constraints. 70% of Indian gamers prefer **English UI** (Lumikai-AWS) even though 90% of new internet users are vernacular-first — split-layer language strategy: English/Hinglish UI, vernacular narrative later.

## 6. The market opening

- India: **~520–555M gamers, #1 in the world by downloads**, $1.5B video-games revenue (2025) growing 17% through the real-money-gaming ban; projected $3.2B by 2030 (Lumikai; FICCI-EY 2026).
- **The Aug 2025 Online Gaming Act killed real-money gaming and explicitly favors "educational and social games"** (18% GST vs. 40% sin-tax) — a mindset game sits precisely in the state-blessed category. Capital, talent, and attention are rotating into casual/mid-core.
- **Demand for cultural themes is proven**: 82% of Indian gamers want Indian-mythology themes; 43% of non-gamers would start for Ramayana-based games; Lumikai's FY24 report literally names **"digital decolonisation"** as a macro consumer trend.
- **Cautionary tales**: FAU-G — nationalist branding drove record pre-registrations, boring gameplay killed it (rating 2.3, delisted). Azadi Quest — government edutainment, no compelling loop, vanished. **Identity converts installs; only the loop retains.**
- **Templates that work**: Ludo King (1.25B downloads, ~80% ad revenue — culturally familiar play); Venba (BAFTA — intimate cultural specificity, not epic nationalism); STAGE (4.4M paying subscribers for dialect content — **Bharat pays for identity content** at low price points via UPI); PhysicsWallah (affordability beat BYJU'S brand).
- **The gap**: every notable decolonial game worldwide (Never Alone, When Rivers Were Trails, LaPensée's corpus) is North-American Indigenous, grant-funded, classroom/PC-first. **There is no mobile-first, free-to-play, mass-market decolonial-mindset game anywhere — and none for South Asia.**

## 7. Implications carried into the design

1. The enemy has **two heads**: internalized inferiority (cringe) *and* hollow supremacism (bluster). Nandy and Táíwò supply the second axis most "patriotic" products miss. → The Mindset Compass.
2. **Respect real trade-offs**: English genuinely pays (+34%); a game that punishes learning English is lying. The dilemma is *English-instead-of* vs. *English-and*. Adding, not replacing.
3. **Facts as collectibles, forgeries as gameplay**: India's real receipts are strong enough that spotting counterfeits (Vedic aircraft) can be a core mechanic — discernment made playable, misinformation hygiene as a bonus.
4. **Individual → national causality must be visible**: sanitation, UPI adoption, women working, air quality — each has a documented chain to national outcomes; show it, every time.
5. **Daily ritual + shareable artifact + streaks** for the multi-session exposure the meta-analyses demand.
6. **≤~40% on-message content**; the rest is life, humor, cricket, weddings, monsoon — reactance-proofing.
7. **Cross-communal by construction**; no party politics; care around exam stress (support, never depiction; Tele-MANAS 14416).
8. Monetize the Ludo King/STAGE way: free + ads-light + ₹29–299 micro-IAP + identity-content packs; the "educational/social" category is tax-advantaged and grant-eligible (state AVGC-XR policies).
