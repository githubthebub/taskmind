The meditations

First the storm, then the silence.

Osho designed his meditations for people like you — restless, modern, full of undone shouting. You cannot sit still by force, so don’t: exhaust the madness first, and stillness arrives on its own. Choose one. The bell will move you from stage to stage; you only have to be total.

The stop exercise

A bell that interrupts your sleep.

Osho loved Gurdjieff’s trick: in the middle of ordinary life a voice shouts stop! — and you freeze, mid-gesture, mid-thought, like a statue. In that sudden halt the machinery runs on without you, and for one raw moment you can watch it. Leave this page open while you work. At some unguessable moment, the bell will strike. Freeze exactly as you are — don’t swallow, don’t settle into comfort — and simply witness, for one minute.

The book of secrets

One hundred and twelve doors, and every one opens inward.

Five thousand years ago Shiva gave Devi 112 ways in; Osho spent five years walking through them one by one. No belief required — each is an experiment. Draw a door. Try it today, in traffic, in the kitchen, in bed. If nothing happens, draw another; one of the 112 is yours.
