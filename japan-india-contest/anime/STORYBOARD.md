# *Two Lights, One Sky* — storyboard & production plan

**Runtime:** ~25 s (5 shots × ~5 s) + narration over final shot.
**Aspect:** 16:9. No dialogue. Ambient sound design; one closing narration line.
**Pipeline (disclosed, per contest AI policy):** human-authored board → anime-style keyframe
per shot (image model, previous keyframes passed as reference for character/palette
consistency) → image-to-video animation per shot (one primary motion each) → edit + closing
narration (TTS) → final master.

Direction principles per shot are detailed in [`CREATIVE_DIRECTION.md`](CREATIVE_DIRECTION.md).

---

## Shot 1 — "The Wish" (Japan, Tanabata dusk) — 5 s
**Principle:** Miyazaki — *ma*, wind as the first character.
**Frame:** Medium-wide. Hikari (16, short dark hair, summer school uniform) on a wooden
veranda at indigo dusk, reaching up to tie a white *tanzaku* wish-strip onto a bamboo branch
already carrying colorful strips. A wind chime hangs at frame edge. Golden lantern light
from inside the house rims her figure. Painterly clouds, first stars.
**Motion (single):** Wind moves the paper strips and her hair; she completes the tie, hand
lingers.
**Sound:** Evening cicadas, one soft wind-chime note.

## Shot 2 — "The Flutter" (insert, Japan) — 4 s
**Principle:** Satoshi Kon — outgoing half of the match cut.
**Frame:** Close-up, center-frame: the white tanzaku with handwritten wish (「また会えます
ように」 – "may we meet again") fluttering against the deep indigo sky, bamboo leaves
soft-focus around it.
**Motion (single):** The strip's rhythmic flutter — tempo we will match in Shot 3's flame.
**Cut point:** On a flutter-beat, white shape center frame.

## Shot 3 — "The Flame" (India, Diwali night) — 5 s
**Principle:** Kon (incoming match) + Naoko Yamada — hands before faces.
**Frame:** Close-up, center-frame: a clay *diya*'s flame flickering in the same screen
position and rhythm as the tanzaku. Pull back slightly: Diya's hands (glass bangles, a
smudge of rangoli powder on one knuckle) shield the flame, then place the lamp into a row of
diyas along a terracotta rooftop parapet. Her face stays above frame — hands carry the
feeling.
**Motion (single):** Flame flicker; hands set the lamp down.
**Sound:** Distant firecrackers, night breeze.

## Shot 4 — "Her Sky" (India → the sky) — 5 s
**Principle:** Makoto Shinkai — the sky as the space between people.
**Frame:** Wide from behind: Diya (16, braid, teal-and-gold salwar kameez) at the parapet of
a Jaipur rooftop — water tank, drying laundry, distant fort silhouette — the city below
strung with lamp-light. She looks up. Camera tilts up into a deep blue-violet night sky
where fireworks bloom small and far away among the stars.
**Motion (single):** Slow upward tilt; fireworks blossom.

## Shot 5 — "One Sky" (title card) — 6 s
**Principle:** Shinkai's shared sky + the contest thesis.
**Frame:** One continuous night sky: the Milky Way (Tanabata's river of stars) flowing into
Diwali's drifting lamp-glow and distant fireworks. At the bottom corners, two small rooftop
silhouettes — Hikari's veranda on the left, Diya's parapet on the right — both figures
looking up at the same sky. Title text appears center: **TWO LIGHTS, ONE SKY**, with
「ふたつの光、ひとつの空」 and "दो रोशनी, एक आसमान" beneath, then small: *Kizuna–Bandhan:
The Japan–India Cross-Cultural Understanding Contest*.
**Motion (single):** Gentle star shimmer / lamp-light drift; text holds steady.
**Narration (EN master):** "Two lights. One sky. Show us the world through each other's eyes."

## Character sheet

| | **Hikari** (光) | **Diya** (दीया) |
|---|---|---|
| Age | 16 | 16 |
| Look | Short dark hair, navy-and-white summer school uniform | Long braid, teal salwar kameez with gold trim, glass bangles |
| Setting | Wooden veranda, small Japanese town, Tanabata | Terracotta rooftop, Jaipur, Diwali |
| Palette | Indigo dusk, paper-white, bamboo green, warm lantern gold | New-moon blue, marigold orange, terracotta, flame gold |

Both girls' names mean "light." The shared warm-gold accent (lantern ↔ diya flame) is what
makes the Shot 2→3 match cut read as one continuous thought.

## Edit notes

- Hard cut S2→S3 exactly on a flutter/flicker beat — this cut is the film.
- S1 and S4 may breathe (hold 0.5 s longer) if total runtime allows; never trim S2/S3.
- Audio crossfade: cicadas/chime → firecrackers/breeze across the match cut; both beds fall
  to near-silence for the title, leaving room for the narration line.
