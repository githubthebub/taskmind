offline · client-side · camera-driven biofeedback
What this is: a real, working closed-loop biofeedback toy. Your camera estimates pulse from micro-color changes in your skin (rPPG) using an in-browser FFT; the sound, visuals, and on-screen cues adapt to that signal. Choose your Lead mode (follow the pacer, or let the app follow you), set the pace, and optionally turn on the mic so it can hear an aum hum and your breath. Every input cross-checks the optical read against your real breathing (heart rate should rise on the inhale, fall on the exhale) and drives the confidence bar and ± range on every number. What it is not: a medical device. Webcam heart-rate — and especially HRV — is a rough estimate; the confidence bar tells you how much to trust it, and low confidence means treat the numbers as noise. Nothing here diagnoses or treats anything. Nothing leaves your device.
