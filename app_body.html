offline · client-side · camera-driven biofeedback
What this is: a real, working closed-loop biofeedback toy. Your camera estimates pulse from micro-color changes in your skin (rPPG) using an in-browser FFT; the sound, visuals, and on-screen cues adapt to that signal. What it is not: a medical device. Webcam heart-rate — and especially HRV — is a rough estimate, easily thrown off by lighting and motion. Nothing here diagnoses, treats, or measures your nervous system clinically. Nothing leaves your device.
